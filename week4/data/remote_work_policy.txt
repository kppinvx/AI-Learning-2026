REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

Remote work is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Employees must remain available during normal working hours while working
remotely.

Employees must have a reliable internet connection and a suitable workspace.

Remote work is not permitted when an employee is required to attend an
in-person client meeting unless the manager provides an exception.

Employees working remotely must follow all company information security
requirements.

Confidential company information must not be accessed from unsecured public
Wi-Fi networks.
