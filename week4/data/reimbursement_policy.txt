EXPENSE AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

Original receipts or valid digital receipts must be attached to the claim.

Travel expenses require prior manager approval unless the expense is related
to an emergency business requirement.

Meals during approved business travel may be reimbursed up to the applicable
company limit.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

Employees must provide accurate information when submitting expense claims.

Finance may reject claims that do not contain sufficient documentation.
