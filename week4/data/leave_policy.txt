LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Employees who have completed less than one year of service receive leave on a
pro-rata basis.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 7 calendar days before
the requested start date.

Emergency leave may be requested without the 7-day notice requirement.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the
following calendar year.

Unused carried-forward leave expires at the end of March of the following year.

Medical leave requires a medical certificate when the absence exceeds 2
consecutive working days.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.
