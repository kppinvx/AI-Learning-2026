WORKING HOURS POLICY

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday.

Normal working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

Employees are expected to maintain punctuality and attendance.

Employees arriving more than 15 minutes late should notify their manager.

Flexible working hours may be approved by the manager based on team and
business requirements.

Employees may be required to work outside normal hours during critical
production incidents or urgent business requirements.
